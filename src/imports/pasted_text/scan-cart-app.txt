# APP: SCANCART

ScanCart is a mobile-first barcode shopping and nutrition assistant.

The main interaction is:

**SCAN → IDENTIFY → ANALYZE → VERIFY → ADD TO CART → SCAN MORE → CHECKOUT**

The user scans a product barcode.

The app identifies the product.

It then displays whatever product information is available, including:

* Product name
* Brand
* Calories
* Protein
* Carbohydrates
* Fat
* Serving size
* MRP
* Ingredients
* Expiry date
* Product category

The app must clearly distinguish between information obtained from a product database, information detected through OCR, information entered by the user, and unavailable information.

## IMPORTANT BARCODE LOGIC

Do NOT pretend that a normal barcode directly contains calories, nutrition information, expiry date, or MRP.

The barcode should be treated as a product identifier.

Use a local mock product database for the prototype.

Expiry dates should be handled separately through an expiry-date OCR simulation or manual entry.

MRP can come from product data or package OCR.

If information is unavailable, display:

**Not available**

Do not invent missing information.
